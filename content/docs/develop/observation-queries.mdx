These are the five observation slices in the current public contract. Each tab is one slice. The choice is stored in this browser. The matrix under the tabs is the supported and rejected temporal parameters, checked against `https://api.onehealthatlas.org` on 18 October 2026. The release id on the successful rows was `governed-2026-10-15-january-climate-prod`.

County examples use FIPS `36061` (New York County, New York). Atlas product geography for these guides is the lower 48 states and the District of Columbia. Preserve leading zeros.

Discover ids before you copy one. `GET /v1/measures?page_size=100` on that date returned 25 measures and `next_page_token` null. A later release can add or remove ids. Read `temporal_semantics` on the measure you selected. The generated parameter list stays on [Observations](/docs/api-reference/observations). The machine-readable document is the [OpenAPI schema](https://api.onehealthatlas.org/openapi.json).

    ### Annual surveillance

    Published human surveillance in this check is the 2023 case-count floor, measure `case_count_floor_2023`. It is a privacy-protected floor in `cases`. It is not a diagnosis, a complete incidence count, or an individual risk estimate.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=case_count_floor_2023&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&year=2023" />

    That request returned HTTP 200 and one row: `temporal_grain` `YEAR`, period 2023-01-01 through 2023-12-31, `value_state` `OBSERVED`, a numeric `value`, `unit` `cases`, and null `environmental_context` and `surveillance_context`. Read the number from your response.

    The same county and measure with `start_date` 2023-01-01 and `end_date` 2023-12-31, and no `year`, returned the same kind of row. Sending `year` together with both dates returned HTTP 400 `INVALID_REQUEST` and detail `Use year or a date range, not both.` Omitting the period returned HTTP 400 `INVALID_REQUEST` and detail `Supply a year or both start_date and end_date.`

    `year` 2022 returned HTTP 200, `data` empty, meta `availability` `UNAVAILABLE`, and `reason` `NO_PUBLISHED_RESULTS`. That empty page is not a 2022 surveillance slice and not a zero. `vintage_year` returned HTTP 400 `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` and detail `This measure does not support vintage_year.` A stratification value of age returned HTTP 400 `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION` and detail `This release has no governed strata.`

    ### Cumulative vector and pathogen

    The checked vector measure is `scapularis_status`. The checked pathogen measure is `burgdorferi_status`. The catalog also lists `pacificus_status`. This page does not treat that third id as checked. Both checked measures use one cumulative day, `start_date` and `end_date` 2025-12-31. `temporal_grain` on the rows was `CUMULATIVE`. That day is not an annual collection event.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=scapularis_status&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&start_date=2025-12-31&end_date=2025-12-31" />

    The scapularis request returned HTTP 200 and one row: publisher value Established, `value_state` `OBSERVED`, `unit` `status`, source id tick, and `surveillance_context.evidence.normalized_state` `established`. The same dates for `burgdorferi_status` returned HTTP 200, publisher value No records, `value_state` `NO_RECORDS`, source id pathogen, and `normalized_state` `unknown`. `No records` does not establish absence. County status does not establish abundance, prevalence, or individual risk.

    `year` 2025 on `scapularis_status` returned HTTP 400 `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` and detail `CDC status is cumulative as published; omit year and use start_date=end_date=2025-12-31.` A range from 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31 returned the same code and detail. `vintage_year` returned HTTP 400 `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` and detail `This measure does not support vintage_year.` Missing dates returned HTTP 400 `INVALID_REQUEST` and detail `Supply a year or both start_date and end_date.` A stratification value of species returned HTTP 400 `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION`.

## Temporal parameters

Supported means the checked request returned HTTP 200 and a row, or, where noted, HTTP 200 and an explicit empty page. Rejected means HTTP 400 before a row. Empty `data` is not zero and does not establish absence.

| Slice | `year` | `start_date` and `end_date` | `vintage_year` | `stratification` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Annual surveillance | Supported for 2023. 2022 was HTTP 200 and an empty page | Supported for 2023-01-01 through 2023-12-31. Both `year` and the dates together are rejected | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION` |
| Cumulative vector and pathogen | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` | Supported only when both dates are 2025-12-31. A wider 2025 range was rejected | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION` |
| January 2025 environment | Supported for 2025 as 31 daily January rows, not one annual value | Supported for one January day and for 1 January through 31 January. 1 February was HTTP 200 and an empty page | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION` |
| 2022 SVI and ACS | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | Required. 2022 is supported. 2023 is rejected | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION` |
| 2023 RUCC | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | Required. 2023 is supported. 2022 is rejected | Rejected. `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION` |

There is no caller sort and no aggregation. `sort`, `aggregation`, and an unknown parameter are `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` on the observations operation. See [Indicators, measures, and observations](/docs/develop/resources).

## Slices the contract does not support

These are not additional slices. Do not fill them in from another measure.

- An annual surveillance year other than 2023. The checked 2022 request was an empty page with `NO_PUBLISHED_RESULTS`.
- A CDC `year`, or any CDC date range other than both dates equal to 2025-12-31.
- An environmental day outside January 2025. `year` 2025 does not mean a full calendar year.
- `vintage_year` on annual surveillance, cumulative status, or the January environmental measures.
- `year` or a date range on SVI, ACS, or RUCC context. Those measures reject annual and day filters.
- A vintage other than 2022 for SVI and ACS, or other than 2023 for RUCC.
- Any `stratification`. The checked release has no governed strata.
- Counties outside the lower 48 and the District of Columbia. The environmental measure limitation says coverage outside the contiguous set is unavailable. These guides do not add a request for that coverage.

Land-cover measures are a different annual series. They are not this environmental slice. See [Workflow guides](/docs/develop/workflows).
