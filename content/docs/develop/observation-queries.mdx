Choose a checked slice and send only its time parameter. The browser stores the tab. Successful rows use release `governed-2026-10-15-january-climate-prod`.

Examples use FIPS `36061` (New York County, New York). Product geography is the lower 48 states and the District of Columbia. Preserve leading zeros. Atlas results are not a diagnosis. A percentile, status, or floor is not individual risk. An empty page is not a zero. See [Evidence, provenance, and uncertainty](/docs/evidence-and-uncertainty).

A sample `GET /v1/measures?page_size=100` returned 25 measures and `next_page_token` null. A later release can change ids. Read `temporal_semantics`. Parameters are on [Observations](/docs/api-reference/observations) and in the [OpenAPI schema](https://api.onehealthatlas.org/openapi.json).

    ### Annual surveillance

    `case_count_floor_2023` is a privacy-protected floor in `cases`.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=case_count_floor_2023&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&year=2023" />

    Sample: HTTP 200, one row, `temporal_grain` `YEAR`, 2023-01-01 through 2023-12-31, `OBSERVED`, a numeric `value`, `unit` `cases`, null `environmental_context` and `surveillance_context`. The same dates without `year` returned the same kind of row. `year` 2022 returned HTTP 200, empty `data`, `availability` `UNAVAILABLE`, and `reason` `NO_PUBLISHED_RESULTS`.

  </Tab>
  <Tab forceMount value="Cumulative vector and pathogen">
    ### Cumulative vector and pathogen

    Checked measures: `scapularis_status` and `burgdorferi_status`. `pacificus_status` is in the catalog and was not checked. Both checked measures use `start_date` and `end_date` 2025-12-31. `temporal_grain` was `CUMULATIVE`, a published cumulative point rather than an annual collection.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=scapularis_status&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&start_date=2025-12-31&end_date=2025-12-31" />

    Scapularis sample: HTTP 200, publisher value Established, `OBSERVED`, `unit` `status`, source tick, `surveillance_context.evidence.normalized_state` `established`. Burgdorferi on the same dates: HTTP 200, publisher value No records, `NO_RECORDS`, source pathogen, `normalized_state` `unknown`. `No records` does not establish absence. County status does not establish abundance or prevalence. `year` 2025, and 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31, returned HTTP 400 `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` and detail `CDC status is cumulative as published; omit year and use start_date=end_date=2025-12-31.`

  </Tab>
  <Tab forceMount value="January 2025 environment">
    ### January 2025 environment

    Checked measure: `nclimgrid_tavg_county_day` on `climate_temperature`. `nclimgrid_tmax_county_day`, `nclimgrid_tmin_county_day`, and `nclimgrid_prcp_county_day` were not the checked observation. The limitation is January 2025 descriptive weather only, with no Lyme causal, risk, or ML claim. `environmental_context` is coverage for that weather value.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=nclimgrid_tavg_county_day&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&start_date=2025-01-17&end_date=2025-01-17" />

    Sample: HTTP 200, grain DAY, 2025-01-17, `OBSERVED`, unit degree Celsius, `environmental_context.coverage_status` `COMPLETE`. The labeled 24-hour period ends in the early morning and is not a midnight calendar day. `year` 2025 returned 31 daily rows, 2025-01-01 through 2025-01-31, not one annual value. 1 January through 31 January also returned 31 rows. 2025-02-01 returned HTTP 200, empty `data`, `UNAVAILABLE`, and `NO_PUBLISHED_RESULTS`.

  </Tab>
  <Tab forceMount value="Land cover">
    ### Land cover

    `nlcd_forest_area_share_county_year` on `land_cover_context` uses unit `fraction` and temporal semantics 2025. A true zero stays zero. An unselected county has no observation. Limitations name TIGER geographies 09110 and 51013 only. This is separate from January weather.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=nlcd_forest_area_share_county_year&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&year=2025" />

    FIPS 36061 is outside that set. The sample, the 2025 date range without `year`, and `year` 2023 each returned HTTP 200, empty `data`, `UNAVAILABLE`, and `NO_PUBLISHED_RESULTS`. A selected geography is a different request. See [Workflow guides](/docs/develop/workflows).

  </Tab>
  <Tab forceMount value="2022 SVI and ACS">
    ### 2022 SVI and ACS

    Send `vintage_year` 2022. Checked measure: `svi_percentile_2022`. `population_2022` and `uninsured_percent_2022` also returned HTTP 200. The period is 2018-01-01 through 2022-12-31, `temporal_grain` `PERIOD`, one window rather than five annual observations. A percentile rank is not a percentage of people.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=svi_percentile_2022&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&vintage_year=2022" />

    Sample: HTTP 200, `OBSERVED`, unit percentile, `county_context.vintage_year` 2022, `county_context.source_field` `RPL_THEMES`, `category_label` null. `environmental_context` and `surveillance_context` were null. `year` 2022, the ACS dates, and a request with no time parameter each returned HTTP 400 `INVALID_REQUEST` and detail `Supply vintage_year for county context.` `vintage_year` 2023 returned HTTP 400 `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` and detail `Unsupported county context time selection.`

  </Tab>
  <Tab forceMount value="2023 RUCC">
    ### 2023 RUCC

    Send `vintage_year` 2023 and `rucc_2023`. The code is a vintage category, not an annual rate or an ordered distance. Period dates on the checked row were null.

    <ObservationCurl query="measure_id=rucc_2023&geography_type=county&geography_id=36061&vintage_year=2023" />

    Sample: HTTP 200, `temporal_grain` `VINTAGE_YEAR`, `value` 1, `OBSERVED`, `unit` `code`, `county_context.vintage_year` 2023, `county_context.source_field` `RUCC_2023`, `category_label` `Metro: area population at least 1 million`. Codes 1 through 9 are vintage-specific. Do not substitute an older vintage. `year` 2023 and the 2023 calendar dates returned detail `Supply vintage_year for county context.` `vintage_year` 2022 returned detail `Unsupported county context time selection.`

## Choose a time parameter

Supported means HTTP 200 with a row or, where noted, an explicit empty page. Rejected means HTTP 400 before a row. Empty `data` is not zero and does not establish absence. `vintage_year` on surveillance, vector, environment, and land cover returns `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER` and detail `This measure does not support vintage_year.` A missing period on those slices returns `INVALID_REQUEST` and detail `Supply a year or both start_date and end_date.` Sending `year` with both dates returns detail `Use year or a date range, not both.` Any `stratification` returns `UNSUPPORTED_STRATIFICATION`. The annual age check used detail `This release has no governed strata.`

| Slice | `year` | `start_date` and `end_date` | `vintage_year` | `stratification` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Annual surveillance | 2023 supported. 2022 was an empty page | 2023-01-01 through 2023-12-31. Not with `year` | Rejected | Rejected |
| Cumulative vector and pathogen | Rejected | Only both dates 2025-12-31. A wider 2025 range was rejected | Rejected | Rejected |
| January 2025 environment | 2025 means 31 January days, not one annual value | One January day, or 1–31 January. 1 February was an empty page | Rejected | Rejected |
| Land cover | 2025 and 2023 accepted as empty pages for FIPS 36061 | 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31 was an empty page. Not with `year` | Rejected | Rejected |
| 2022 SVI and ACS | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | 2022 supported. 2023 rejected | Rejected |
| 2023 RUCC | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | Rejected. `INVALID_REQUEST` | 2023 supported. 2022 rejected | Rejected |

You cannot sort or aggregate. `sort`, `aggregation`, and an unknown parameter are `UNSUPPORTED_FILTER`. See [Indicators, measures, and observations](/docs/develop/resources).

## Leave unsupported selections empty

- Surveillance years other than 2023. The 2022 sample was empty, `NO_PUBLISHED_RESULTS`.
- A CDC `year`, or any CDC range other than both dates 2025-12-31.
- An environmental day outside January 2025.
- `year` or a date range on SVI, ACS, or RUCC.
- A vintage other than 2022 for SVI and ACS, or 2023 for RUCC.
- Any `stratification`. The checked release has no governed strata.
- Counties outside the lower 48 and the District of Columbia. Coverage outside that set is unavailable. Do not add a request for it.

January environment is weather. Land cover is the annual series. An unselected county stays an empty page.
