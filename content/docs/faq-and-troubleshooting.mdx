Epidemiologists use this page to answer a common interpretation or workspace question, then open the guide that owns the longer explanation.

## Product and interpretation

### What is One Health Atlas?

Atlas is an **Epidemiologist Decision Intelligence Platform** for Lyme disease and other tick-borne diseases. It brings One Health evidence together so you can see what is happening and decide what deserves review. Start with [What is One Health Atlas?](/docs/what-is-atlas), then open [Atlas Explorer](/explore).

### Is the current Atlas score an AI prediction?

No. It is a transparent, adjustable follow-up-priority calculation over released inputs. It is not a predictive model, diagnosis, disease-burden estimate, causal conclusion, or individual-risk prediction. See [evidence guidance](/docs/evidence-and-uncertainty).

### Does Atlas connect to an electronic health record or a clinical laboratory?

No. The current release is open public data. A future adapter is not a live EHR connection, a TEFCA connection, patient-level data, or a production clinical-laboratory feed. Read [sources and canonical evidence](/docs/sources-and-canonical-evidence).

### Does a high score mean that a county is high risk?

No. The released inputs or evidence gaps made the county a review candidate under the displayed methodology. Check source, vintage, geography, method, completeness, limitations, and uncertainty first.

### Why does Atlas show a signal when evidence is missing?

Missing can be a reason to review a question. Missing is not zero, and no record is not absence. A low observed value and insufficient surveillance are different states. Read [evidence, provenance, and uncertainty](/docs/evidence-and-uncertainty).

### Can Atlas tell me whether an outbreak will happen?

No. Atlas does not guarantee outbreak prediction, causation, individual risk, diagnosis, or automatic public-health action. It helps you name a bounded investigation question.

### What does “AI-enabled” mean today?

It is the direction: organize, compare, synthesize, or explain governed evidence with sources and limits. The public score is deterministic. When literature chat is enabled, `/assistant` calls the model-backed literature service and returns cited evidence. When it is off, the assistant says the service is unavailable. See [AI-enabled decision intelligence](/docs/ai-enabled-decision-intelligence) and the [AI Ethics commitments](/ai-ethics).

## Using Atlas

### The Atlas is loading or says it is temporarily unavailable. What should I do?

Use **Try again** or refresh once. If it continues, record the time, route, and any release or request reference. A loading or error state is not evidence about a county.

### No counties match my filters. Did Atlas find no risk?

No. The filters may exclude every county. Review state, name or FIPS search, evidence filter, view, and score controls, then use **Clear filters**. An empty result is not a public-health conclusion.

### The map is unavailable or difficult to use. Can I still review results?

Yes. In [Atlas Explorer](/explore), use the county list and the text summaries. The list is the non-map path.

### How do I compare counties?

In [Compare](/compare), choose two counties from the same release. The comparison is descriptive. Do not treat a difference as causation or individual risk. Atlas does not offer a five-county comparison page.

### Why is a county’s case value not shown as zero?

The source may have no county-linked record, suppressed or unallocated records, or no report for that geography or period. Atlas does not convert missing evidence into zero. A published zero is still bounded by the source and method.

### Why can two signals point in different directions?

Sources can differ in boundaries, definitions, sampling, periods, publishers, and update schedules. Compare provenance first. Do not average away the difference.

### How do I share what I reviewed?

Share the URL or export, and record the release, methodology version, geography, filters, and limitations. A URL preserves supported analytical state. A later release may differ.

## HelpDocs and policy

### How do I find the right documentation page?

Open the [documentation home](/docs) and search. **Docs** opens this site in a new tab and leaves the workspace URL on the original tab.

### I am in the professional workspace. Which page should I use?

Use [Atlas Explorer](/explore), [Compare](/compare), and [Ask Atlas](/askatlas) for their own jobs. Saved chats stay in this browser. The request is a message plus optional conversation history. There is no Structured mode and no Both mode. Feed lists trusted-source items. An empty, disabled, or failed feed is its own state. Settings saves a default jurisdiction. See [the professional workspace](/docs/professional-workspace).

### Can I create an API key in Settings?

No. Public reads do not use an API key, and Settings does not generate one. Use [API, MCP, and access](/docs/api-mcp-and-access), the [developer quickstart](/docs/develop/quickstart), and the [OpenAPI schema](https://api.onehealthatlas.org/openapi.json). Production MCP reads bounded public evidence without a key. The September 2026 smoke-only development receipt is historical. That service is not a live EHR connection.

### Why did my default jurisdiction not change the Review page I already had open?

A saved default applies the next time Atlas Explorer opens without `scope` or `county`. An address that already names a place stays as it is. Optional role, organization, and job title can stay empty.

### Where are privacy and AI policy commitments documented?

Use the [Privacy page](/privacy) and the [AI Ethics commitments](/ai-ethics). HelpDocs links to those sources. It does not replace or expand them.

### Can HelpDocs change a data value or score?

No. HelpDocs explains the release. It does not edit source data, change methodology, or authorize an interpretation.

### What if an AI or analytical explanation conflicts with the evidence panel?

Prefer the governed source and the in-product provenance. Record the disagreement and ask a qualified reviewer to validate it. Treat an unsupported explanation as unavailable. Keep the [AI Ethics commitments](/ai-ethics) in view.
