This page describes the current public Atlas experience. Use it to choose a supported workflow and to avoid treating a planned or experimental idea as a released capability.

## Capability status

Atlas uses four status labels:

- **Available now** — verified in the current public release and appropriate to document as user behavior.
- **Planned** — part of the product direction, but not available to rely on today.
- **Experimental/demo** — available only in a constrained, feature-gated, or validation context; do not treat it as a production commitment.
- **Not supported** — outside the current product promise or deliberately excluded from the public experience.

## What you can do today

| Capability | Status | How it supports investigation | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explore county and state context | Available now | Start with a geography and review its rank, map context, county profile, signals, and evidence together. | A review-priority signal is not a diagnosis, disease-burden estimate, or individual risk estimate. |
| Filter the county set | Available now | Narrow the review to a state, county name or FIPS code, or the available-evidence state. | Filters describe the current release; they do not create new surveillance data. |
| Review geographic views | Available now | Use geographic tiles, small multiples, the evidence matrix, ranked dots, synchronized maps, map-linked scatterplots, county comparison, and the county evidence table to look for patterns worth checking. | The table and text summaries are the non-map path. A map color or rank is a prompt for review, not a conclusion. |
| Compare supported counties | Available now | Select up to five counties from the same release and compare their displayed signals and evidence context side by side. | Comparisons are descriptive and methodology-defined; they do not establish causation or peer-group equivalence. |
| Inspect evidence and provenance | Available now | Open the selected county's evidence and methodology context before deciding what to investigate next. | Check source, geography, time or vintage, release, method version, freshness, completeness, uncertainty, and limitations. |
| Share analytical state | Available now | Share a URL that preserves supported filters, view, metric, selected counties, page, and score assumptions for another reviewer. | Record the release and method with the URL. A URL does not guarantee that a future release will contain identical data. |
| Export a review artifact | Available now | Use the supported county-list CSV or state/county PDF export in the Atlas experience to carry a bounded review into team discussion. | Exports are snapshots of the displayed release and assumptions, not a new analysis or a guarantee of future reproducibility. |

## Authenticated professional workspace

These routes require sign-in. They sit beside the public Explorer until a later cutover. The [professional workspace](/docs/professional-workspace) guide describes each released page.

| Capability | Status | How it supports investigation | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Review at [/app/review](/app/review) | Available now | Start from the United States or one state, and read the map and county list for that scope. | National Review is a bounded picture. It is not a ranking of every U.S. county. A saved default applies only when the address has no scope. |
| Explore at [/app/explore](/app/explore) | Available now | Scan one governed measure on a map and in a table of the same values. | Explore filters stay on Explore. The table remains the non-map path. |
| Investigate at [/app/investigate](/app/investigate) | Available now | Read one county's evidence, limitations, and provenance, then return to Review. | The page opens the county in the address. It does not substitute another county. |
| Compare at [/app/compare](/app/compare) | Available now | Compare two counties on aligned measures, with unit, period, and availability on each value. | This page does not rank a winner. The public Explorer's five-county comparison is a separate legacy surface. |
| Settings at [/app/settings](/app/settings) | Available now | Save United States or a state as the next Review start, plus optional role, organization, and job title. Sign out, export, and removal use the account controls on the same page. | Optional fields can stay empty. The default does not change evidence values or block other geographies. |
| Docs from the workspace | Available now | Open this reference in a new tab from the Access navigation. | The original workspace tab keeps its URL. Docs is not in-page help. |
| Ask Atlas side panel | Experimental/demo | On Review, Explore, Investigate, Compare, and Action, Ask Atlas can request reviewed literature when that service is enabled. | Feature-gated. It does not change the page. With the service off, the panel says Ask Atlas is unavailable. There is no Structured or Both mode. |
| Assistant page at [/app/assistant](/app/assistant) | Experimental/demo | The full research workspace uses the same reviewed-literature conversation as the side panel. **Open full workspace** continues that local chat. | Feature-gated. History stays in this browser. A missing local conversation is recoverable. Structured and Both modes are not available. With the service off, the page says Ask Atlas is unavailable. |
| Action and Feed | Planned | The addresses [/app/action](/app/action) and [/app/feed](/app/feed) are reserved in the navigation. | They do not yet provide follow-up workflows, feed items, or alerts. |
| API keys in Settings | Not supported | Public API reads do not use a key. | Settings does not generate or revoke a key. See [API, MCP, and access](/docs/api-mcp-and-access). |

## Capabilities that are not current promises

| Capability | Status | What this means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical release trends | Not supported | The public explorer currently exposes one active release. A generated date is release metadata, not the date of disease observations. |
| Guaranteed outbreak prediction or automated public-health action | Not supported | Atlas surfaces signals and investigation candidates. It does not predict with certainty or act on behalf of an authority. |
| Clinical diagnosis or person-level exposure risk | Not supported | Atlas is a population-level public-health decision-support experience, not a clinical or individual-risk tool. |
| AI-generated findings treated as authoritative | Experimental/demo | AI-enabled experiences must remain grounded in approved evidence, show limitations, and retain human review. Do not use an AI suggestion as a finding by itself. |
| Alerts, private feeds, or account-based evidence access | Not supported | The public Explorer remains usable without sign-in. A workspace account does not unlock different governed evidence, change a score, or authorize an interpretation. Feed and alerts are not released. |

## Where to start

- Open the [Atlas Explorer](/overview) for county and state review.
- Open the [Geographic Explorer](/geographic_explorer) for coordinated geographic views and the county evidence table.
- Open the signed-in [professional workspace](/docs/professional-workspace) for Review, Explore, Investigate, Compare, and Settings.
- Follow the [investigation workflows](/docs/investigation-workflows) for question-to-next-step guidance.
- Read [evidence, provenance, and uncertainty](/docs/evidence-and-uncertainty) and the methodology context beside the result before relying on a signal. Keep the [AI Ethics commitments](/ai-ethics) nearby for AI-enabled or experimental experiences.
- Read [AI-enabled decision intelligence](/docs/ai-enabled-decision-intelligence) for the status of AI-assisted and future capabilities, or use the [FAQ and troubleshooting guide](/docs/faq-and-troubleshooting) for common questions.

The current release is a situational-awareness foundation. When a question depends on a feed, model, alert, historical comparison, or export that is not listed as available now, treat it as an open product or data dependency and do not infer the answer from the interface.
