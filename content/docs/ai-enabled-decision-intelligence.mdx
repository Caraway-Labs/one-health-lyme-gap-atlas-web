Epidemiologists use this page to see what “AI-enabled” means today, which assistance is released, and which decisions stay in human review.

One Health Atlas is an **Epidemiologist Decision Intelligence Platform**. AI-enabled means help with organizing and explaining governed evidence. It does not replace an epidemiologist or turn a score into a prediction. The framing is:

**signals → evidence → hypotheses → anomaly candidates → investigation priorities**

Each step must remain interpretable, bounded by the evidence, clear about uncertainty, and accountable to human review.

## Capability status

| Capability | Status | What you can rely on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transparent follow-up-priority scoring | Available now | The current score is a deterministic, adjustable calculation over released inputs. It helps focus review; it is not a predictive model, diagnosis, causal claim, disease-burden estimate, or individual-risk output. |
| Model-assisted surveillance review priority | Available now | Signed-in Atlas Explorer can show a published HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW tier when that tier's release matches the selected evidence release. Atlas withholds the tier when the returned release differs, and when the governed release for the page is not known. A withheld, missing, or not-estimable result is not shown as LOW. The tier is review prioritization, not disease risk, predicted incidence, a diagnosis, or an autonomous decision. It is separate from the rules-based county review score. |
| Public evidence and provenance context | Available now | Released signals, source and method context, limitations, and a table path. Start with [evidence, provenance, and uncertainty](/docs/evidence-and-uncertainty). |
| Atlas Assistant: reviewed literature | Early access, feature-gated | `/assistant` asks the API about reviewed PubMed and PMC Open Access literature when enabled, with cited evidence or an unavailable-evidence outcome. It does not query live geography or scores, give medical advice, or replace review. |
| Ask Atlas in the professional workspace | Experimental/demo | Explorer and Compare open [Ask Atlas](/askatlas). Enabled chat uses the same literature service. Disabled chat leaves the other pages usable. The request is a message plus optional history. No Structured mode and no Both mode. |
| Ask Atlas page at `/askatlas` | Experimental/demo | Chats stay in this browser, not on Atlas servers. |
| Broader Atlas evidence assistant | Planned | Mixed data and knowledge-graph questions are not released. |
| Anomaly candidates and interpretable modeling | Planned | Forecasts and anomaly outputs are not released. The review-priority tier is not a forecast. |
| Literature monitoring or agent assistance | Planned | Not released. MCP evidence tools are anonymous reads and do not run an agent. The September 2026 smoke-only development receipt is historical. See [API, MCP, and access](/docs/api-mcp-and-access). |

A planned architecture, a design exploration, or a local demo is not evidence that a capability is available in the public release.

## What human review means

Use an AI-assisted output as a prompt for review. Check source and provenance. Confirm geography, period, coverage, and method. Separate an observation from a derived score or a model. State missing evidence. Choose validation, local review, more analysis, consultation, or no action. If the output cannot show its evidence and limits, treat it as unavailable. Do not infer certainty from fluent language, a rank, a map color, or a generated summary.

Released behavior is in [current capabilities](/docs/current-capabilities) and [investigation workflows](/docs/investigation-workflows).

## What Atlas AI is not for

Atlas does not:

- diagnose Lyme disease or provide clinical or treatment advice;
- predict an individual's exposure, infection, or disease risk;
- guarantee that an outbreak will or will not occur;
- prove that a vector, pathogen, environment, or population factor caused an outcome;
- convert missing surveillance into evidence of absence or zero; or
- make an automatic public-health decision or replace epidemiologists, analysts, local knowledge, or responsible authorities.

The [AI Ethics commitments](/ai-ethics) are the public page for AI status, boundaries, and open decisions. The professional workspace footer opens that same page. Privacy and data-use commitments belong on the [Privacy page](/privacy). This guide does not add to either policy source.
